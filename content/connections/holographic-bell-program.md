## The Link

The [[bell-inequalities-qft|Bell-CHSH program]] constructs dichotomic observables from [[weyl-operators|Weyl operators]] on local algebras of free QFT — exploiting the type III₁ structure of the algebra and the modular-conjugation map between complementary wedges. The same algebraic type appears on the boundary of a holographic CFT in the large-$N$ limit: the algebra of single-trace operators on a boundary subregion is type III₁ ([[large-n-factorization]], [[type-iii-von-neumann-algebras]]). It is therefore natural to ask whether the Bell-CHSH program's tools apply to boundary subalgebras of holographic CFTs, and what — if anything — Bell-CHSH measurements would say about bulk spacetime structure.

## Evidence

- Both settings live on type III₁ factors; the modular conjugation $J$ is defined in both cases and acts between commuting subalgebras (Haag duality in flat space; subregion-subalgebra duality in AdS/CFT — see [[subregion-subalgebra-duality]]).
- [[2025-liu-lectures-entanglement-vna|Liu's 2025 review]] explicitly poses the question of how flat-space algebraic-QFT methods translate to the holographic setting.
- The existing wiki question [[bell-chsh-in-holographic-setting]] frames the same direction from the Bell side.

## Gaps

- No explicit Weyl-operator construction is available for boundary subalgebras of strongly-coupled holographic CFTs. The Weyl algebra requires a Gaussian structure (free or generalized-free fields); at strong coupling, only the large-$N$ generalized-free-field limit provides one.
- Even in the large-$N$ limit, the relation between a Bell-CHSH violation on the boundary and a geometric quantity in the bulk is unclear. Does the Tsirelson bound translate to a bulk inequality?
- Subleading-in-$1/N$ corrections may be crucial for any operational interpretation, and these are not under analytic control.

## Potential Projects

- A first calculation: Bell-CHSH violation for a pair of intervals in a 2d holographic CFT at large central charge, using the generalized-free-field structure. The bulk dual would be a pair of strips in $\mathrm{AdS}_3$ with their RT surfaces; compare the CHSH value to a geometric invariant of the RT configuration.
- Compute the Bell-CHSH violation across a holographic CFT thermofield-double state ([[2026-algebraic-qft-course|AQFT Sem II Wk 8]] already covers Bell-CHSH on TFD in flat space — extend to holographic TFD = eternal AdS-Schwarzschild).
