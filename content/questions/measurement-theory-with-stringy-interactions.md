## Statement

Problem 3.5 of [[2026-goto-rethinking-qi-gravity-fields|Goto et al.]]: how does one formulate a theory of measurement (detector operators, POVMs) when interactions are *stringy* — non-local at the string scale — rather than pointlike? What consequences follow for the "observer as detector" and "observer as operational limitation" viewpoints?

## Why It Matters

Peripheral to the group, but it shares a concern with the group's [[impossible-measurements-qft|impossible-measurements]] question: naive sharp/local measurements are pathological in relativistic and gravitational settings, and both problems ask what a *physically admissible* measurement is. Stringy non-locality is the far-UV version of that caution.

## What We Know

- Unruh–DeWitt detectors model measurement via local field–detector coupling; the group uses these (spin-1 UDW work).
- Sorkin's impossible measurements show ideal projective measurements can violate causality in QFT.
- A stringy/non-local measurement formalism is not established.

## Possible Approaches

- Extend detector models to non-local (smeared over string scale) couplings and test causality.
- Connect to algebraic (POVM-on-a-region) formulations that avoid ideal measurements.
