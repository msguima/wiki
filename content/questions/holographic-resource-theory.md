## Statement

Problem 2.8 of [[2026-goto-rethinking-qi-gravity-fields|Goto et al.]]: what is the gravity dual of a boundary resource theory — i.e. a resource theory whose free states $\mathcal{F}$ are holographic states (those with semiclassical duals), whose free operations $\mathcal{O}$ preserve that class, and whose monotones admit geometric/gravitational descriptions?

## Why It Matters

This is the holographic capstone of Theme 1 and the natural home of the group's most gravitational project: the [[embezzlement-capacity-project|generalized-entropy-as-capacity]] result is a *concrete fragment* of a holographic resource theory — a bulk-geometric monotone ([[quantum-extremal-surfaces|generalized entropy]]) governing an operational rate (holographic [[entanglement-embezzlement|embezzlement]]).

## What We Know

- Free "holographic states" = fixed-area / semiclassical states; entanglement-wedge structure constrains achievable transformations.
- Generalized entropy and entanglement-wedge cross-section already behave as bulk-geometric monotones.
- No complete free-operation/monotone axiomatization exists yet.

## Possible Approaches

- Promote the embezzlement-capacity theorem to a monotone statement in a holographic resource theory.
- Identify the bulk operation dual to a boundary free operation (e.g. area-preserving / RT-preserving maps).
