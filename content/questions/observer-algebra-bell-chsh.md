## Statement

A group-specific spin-off of Theme 2 of [[2026-goto-rethinking-qi-gravity-fields|Goto et al.]] (Problems 3.1/3.4, [[dynamical-observer-backreaction-and-algebra]]): can the group's [[bell-chsh-inequality|Bell-CHSH]] and [[araki-uhlmann-relative-entropy|relative-entropy]] computations be redone on the **type II** "observer's algebra" obtained by adjoining a bounded-below observer Hamiltonian via the [[crossed-product-construction|crossed product]] (CLPW/CPW), rather than on the bare type III₁ algebra?

## Why It Matters

A type II algebra has a trace and well-defined density matrices, so a Bell-CHSH or relative-entropy computation there has a genuinely *operational* observer attached — potentially sidestepping the [[impossible-measurements-qft|impossible-measurements]] worry that shadows sharp measurements in relativistic QFT, and giving the group's numbers a cleaner information-theoretic reading. It ties the group's flat-space toolkit to the observer program the paper's Theme 2 pushes.

## What We Know

- CLPW: adjoining an observer with a bounded-below Hamiltonian turns the (de Sitter / horizon) algebra into type II₁ with a finite maximal-entropy state; the AQFT course develops the free-field analog (Sem II).
- The group already computes Bell-CHSH on the [[thermofield-double-state|TFD]] and vacuum wedge algebras (course Sem II Wk 8).
- What changes when the *dressed* (crossed-product) observables replace the bare ones is not yet worked out.

## Possible Approaches

- Construct dressed cosine-Weyl observables $\hat A, \hat B$ on the crossed-product algebra and recompute the CHSH correlator in the dressed vacuum; compare with the bare-algebra Tsirelson approach.
- Compute a relative entropy between dressed coherent states, using the type II trace, and compare with the type III₁ Araki–Uhlmann result.
