## Statement

Problem 2.1 of [[2026-goto-rethinking-qi-gravity-fields|Goto et al.]]: *can we characterize gravitational observables as operational quantities on the boundary and/or via gravitational tasks?* Diffeomorphism-invariant observables (extremal surfaces/volumes, dressed bulk local operators) are well-defined semiclassically, but it is unclear whether they remain meaningful in full quantum gravity — and whether each corresponds to a concrete boundary information-processing task.

## Why It Matters

This is the umbrella problem of Theme 1 and the general form of a claim the group is already trying to prove in one case: that a geometric bulk quantity has an *operational* meaning. The [[embezzlement-capacity-project|generalized-entropy-as-capacity]] result is precisely an instance — [[quantum-extremal-surfaces|generalized entropy]] characterized as the capacity for holographic [[entanglement-embezzlement|embezzlement]] — so progress here is progress on the group's most holographic project.

## What We Know

- [[ryu-takayanagi-formula|RT]] / [[quantum-extremal-surfaces|QES]] already give entropic (hence semi-operational) readings of extremal surfaces.
- [[subregion-subalgebra-duality|Subregion–subalgebra duality]] and entanglement-wedge reconstruction tie bulk operators to boundary subalgebras.
- A fully task-based dictionary (which task certifies which bulk observable) is missing.

## Possible Approaches

- Prove operational characterizations case by case (generalized entropy ↔ embezzlement capacity; complexity ↔ volume; etc.).
- Use resource theory to attach monotones to bulk quantities — see [[holographic-resource-theory]], [[resource-theory-monotones-in-qft]].
