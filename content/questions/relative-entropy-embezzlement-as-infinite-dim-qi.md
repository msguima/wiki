## Statement

A group-specific spin-off of Problem 5.1 of [[2026-goto-rethinking-qi-gravity-fields|Goto et al.]] ([[quantum-information-in-infinite-dimensions]]): can the group's existing body of results — finite [[araki-uhlmann-relative-entropy|Araki–Uhlmann relative entropy]] for [[coherent-states|coherent]], [[squeezed-states|squeezed]], and [[cat-states|cat]] states, and the type III₁ [[entanglement-embezzlement|embezzlement]] rate/capacity work — be assembled and framed as a concrete contribution to the "quantum information theory in infinite-dimensional Hilbert spaces" the paper calls for?

## Why It Matters

Low technical risk, high visibility. The group has already *computed* the finite information-theoretic quantities that survive on type III₁ algebras — exactly the objects Problem 5.1 says are missing a canonical theory. A perspective/letter that positions the 2025 relative-entropy papers and the [[holographic-dual-embezzlement-protocol|embezzlement-capacity manuscript]] against §5 of Goto et al. would stake a claim on a frontier a large holography/QI collaboration has just publicly prioritized.

## What We Know

- Closed-form relative entropy controlled by the smeared [[pauli-jordan-distribution|Pauli–Jordan distribution]] (coherent states); distinct mass/region scaling for squeezed and two-coherent states; non-trivial dimension dependence (arXiv:2511.20244).
- Embezzlement is exact on type III₁; the group's [[embezzlement-capacity-lower-bound|capacity question]] sharpens its *cost* to $\sim \log d / S_{\text{gen}}$.
- These are precisely "finite QI quantities on infinite-dimensional algebras" — the content §5 asks to systematize.

## Possible Approaches

- Draft a short position note mapping each existing result to a piece of the §5 program (which finite quantities are canonical; which need one-shot/smooth-entropy tooling; where the type II crossed product supplies a trace).
- Identify the *gaps* the group's toolkit does not yet cover (interacting theories — see [[relative-entropy-interacting-theories]]; a general infinite-dim data-processing / resource framework).
