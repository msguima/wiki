## Statement

Problem 2.7 of [[2026-goto-rethinking-qi-gravity-fields|Goto et al.]]: can RG flow be cast as a resource theory, with $c$- and $a$-type quantities (and their holographic counterparts) appearing as **monotones** under coarse-graining treated as a free operation?

## Why It Matters

Adjacent rather than central to the group, but it touches two threads the group cares about: monotonicity of information under coarse-graining (the [[relative-entropy-qft|relative-entropy]] machinery) and the RG/confinement physics of the [[gribov-zwanziger]] program. A resource-theoretic $c$-theorem would be a clean "irreversibility of RG = monotone" statement.

## What We Know

- $c$- (2d), $a$- (4d) theorems establish irreversibility of RG; entropic proofs (Casini–Huerta) already use relative entropy / strong subadditivity.
- Holographically, RG = radial evolution in AdS; monotonicity is geometric (null energy condition).
- A full free-operation/monotone formalization is not standard.

## Possible Approaches

- Identify coarse-graining as a free operation and the $a$-function as the induced monotone.
- Relate to the entropic $c$-theorem, which is already relative-entropy-based.
