## Statement

Problem 4.4 of [[2026-goto-rethinking-qi-gravity-fields|Goto et al.]]: which specific features of quantum error correction are actually *needed* to describe quantum gravity? Given non-isometric bulk-to-boundary maps and only-approximate holographic codes, does QG require full QI protection, or only a relaxed version?

## Why It Matters

Peripheral to the group's programs, but conceptually adjacent to the [[type-iii-von-neumann-algebras|type III₁]] / operator-algebra thread: operator-algebra QEC (OAQECC) is the infinite-dimensional generalization that links QEC to the algebras the group works with — so this problem shares mathematical furniture with [[quantum-information-in-infinite-dimensions]].

## What We Know

- Holographic codes are approximate and, at finite $G$, non-isometric (Akers–Penington).
- Entanglement-wedge reconstruction is operator-algebra QEC.
- "Full protection" (exact QEC) is likely too strong; the useful notion is approximate/algebraic.

## Possible Approaches

- Identify the minimal QEC property that reproduces holographic reconstruction.
- Formulate via OAQECC on von Neumann algebras.
