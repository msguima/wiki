## Statement

Problem 4.2 of [[2026-goto-rethinking-qi-gravity-fields|Goto et al.]]: toy models of QG routinely assume dynamics are approximately Haar-random. What properties of physical (local, time-independent) many-body Hamiltonian dynamics are necessary and sufficient to reproduce near-Haar behavior (unitary designs) relevant for scrambling and encoding?

## Why It Matters

Peripheral to the group; a quantum-information / many-body-chaos question. Nearest group contact is erick-landim's many-body-theory interest, but not a current line.

## What We Know

- Haar-random unitaries and unitary $k$-designs reproduce scrambling; local random circuits form designs in polynomial depth.
- Recent work: constant-local time-independent Hamiltonian ensembles cannot form accurate unitary 2-designs; polylog-local ones can approach design behavior.

## Possible Approaches

- Characterize the Hamiltonian-locality thresholds for approximate-design formation.
- Relate design-formation time to scrambling / OTOC decay.
