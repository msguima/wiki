## Statement

Problem 4.3 of [[2026-goto-rethinking-qi-gravity-fields|Goto et al.]]: an "inverse Hayden–Preskill" question — rather than recovering an input state from radiation given a known interior unitary, can *features of the interior dynamics themselves* be inferred from the emitted radiation?

## Why It Matters

Peripheral to the group; a learning-theory / black-hole question. Included for completeness of Theme 3.

## What We Know

- Standard Hayden–Preskill assumes the interior unitary is known.
- Hamiltonian learning and process tomography give finite-dim analogs of "learning the dynamics from outputs."

## Possible Approaches

- Frame as quantum process learning with restricted (radiation-only) access.
- Bound learnability by scrambling / design properties (links to [[many-body-dynamics-for-near-haar-encoding]]).
