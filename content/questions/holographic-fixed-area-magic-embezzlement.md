## Statement

In the group's holographic [[entanglement-embezzlement|embezzlement]] program, **fixed-area states** serve as the flat-spectrum reference against which the [[holographic-dual-embezzlement-protocol|embezzlement protocol]] and its [[embezzlement-capacity-lower-bound|capacity]] are defined. [[2026-benedetti-magic-in-qft|Benedetti–Dabholkar–Dalmonte]] identify fixed-area states as **flat-spectrum, stabilizer-like** tensor-network states, and show that physical (cyclic-separating) bulk states acquire non-flat Rényi corrections — i.e. [[magic-nonstabilizerness|magic]] — via the modified cosmic-brane prescription. **Is the embezzlement cost / capacity of a holographic state quantitatively related to its magic — its departure from the nearest fixed-area (stabilizer) state?**

## Why It Matters

It would tie the group's most developed holographic result — generalized entropy as the operational embezzlement capacity — to the newly-imported magic resource, using an object both frameworks already privilege: the **fixed-area state**. Embezzlement in [[type-iii-von-neumann-algebras|type III₁]] is "costless" in the strict algebraic sense but has a relative-entropy cost operationally; magic measures distance from the flat/fixed-area sector. If the embezzlement cost and the magic are controlled by the *same* departure-from-fixed-area quantity, then generalized entropy (the group's [[embezzlement-capacity-lower-bound|capacity]] candidate) and magic corrections (the cosmic-brane subleading terms) are two views of one bulk quantity — a concrete instance of the Goto et al. call for a [[holographic-resource-theory|holographic resource theory]] with more than one resource in play.

## What We Know

- **Fixed-area states are the shared reference.** Flat spectrum, stabilizer-like, and the base of both the cosmic-brane Rényi computation ([[2026-benedetti-magic-in-qft|the paper]], via Dong) and the group's embezzlement construction ([[holographic-dual-embezzlement-protocol]]).
- **Magic = non-flat correction.** The paper writes any cyclic-separating state in the fixed-area basis and reads its magic off the non-flat subleading Rényi contribution — the same expansion that controls holographic Rényi entropies.
- **Embezzlement cost is a relative entropy.** The group measures embezzlement cost in [[araki-uhlmann-relative-entropy|Araki–Uhlmann relative entropy]] ([[embezzlement-cost-relative-entropy]]); relating it to magic needs the [[relative-entropy-magic-inequality|magic ↔ relative-entropy]] link.
- **Type II tensor-network context.** The relevant infinite-tensor-network / type II analysis (Chemissany–Gesteau et al. 2025, cited by the paper) is the same machinery behind the crossed-product entropy the group uses.

## Possible Approaches

1. **Magic of fixed-area vs. physical states.** Compute the anti-flatness of a holographic state expanded in the fixed-area basis (cosmic-brane subleading term) and compare its scaling to the embezzlement cost of the same state.
2. **Capacity ↔ magic dictionary.** Test whether the generalized-entropy capacity from [[embezzlement-capacity-lower-bound]] and the magic correction share a common bulk quantity (e.g. the cosmic-brane backreaction / area variance).
3. **RT/QES reference.** Use [[ryu-takayanagi-formula|RT]] / [[quantum-extremal-surfaces|QES]] fixed-area states as the flat baseline and track how bulk matter (which makes the state physical/magical) simultaneously turns on embezzlement cost.
4. **Resource-theory packaging.** Frame fixed-area states as the "free states" and magic + embezzlement as two monotones, answering part of [[holographic-resource-theory]] / [[resource-theory-monotones-in-qft]] with a worked holographic example.
