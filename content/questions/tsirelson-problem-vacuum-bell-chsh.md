## Statement

A group-specific spin-off of Theme 4 of [[2026-goto-rethinking-qi-gravity-fields|Goto et al.]], which flags the **Tsirelson problem** — whether the set of quantum correlations defined by *tensor-product* measurements equals that defined by *commuting-operator* measurements — as the sharp infinite-dimensional subtlety behind nonlocal games (resolved negatively at the operator-algebra level by $\mathrm{MIP}^\ast=\mathrm{RE}$). Question: does this distinction affect the group's [[bell-chsh-inequality|Bell-CHSH]] results in QFT, where Alice's and Bob's observables live in *commuting* wedge/diamond [[type-iii-von-neumann-algebras|von Neumann algebras]] that do **not** tensor-factorize?

## Why It Matters

The group's Bell-CHSH-in-QFT program is built on [[weyl-operators|Weyl operators]] in commuting spacelike algebras with [[tomita-takesaki-modular-theory|modular]]-conjugation-related observables — i.e. it lives natively on the *commuting-operator* side of the Tsirelson dichotomy, not the tensor-product side that finite-dimensional Bell intuition assumes. Whether the Tsirelson gap constrains the achievable violation (or its decay) is the algebraic crux of the [[bell-in-type-iii-project|type-III₁ Bell chapter]] (OQP #1/26/32) and bears on the [[long-range-bell-decay|β(L) decay law]] (OQP #12).

## What We Know

- Local QFT algebras satisfy microcausality (spacelike-commuting), and the vacuum is cyclic/separating (Reeh–Schlieder) — the setting is commuting-operator, and generically does not admit a Hilbert-space tensor factorization across the cut.
- Summers–Werner: the vacuum maximally violates Bell-CHSH ($2\sqrt2$) on suitable wedge algebras — a commuting-algebra statement.
- The group achieves numerical values $\sim 2.7$–$2.8$ (bumpified Haar wavelets) approaching Tsirelson; these are commuting-algebra constructions.
- Tsirelson equality holds in finite dimensions; the gap is an infinite-dimensional/operator-algebraic phenomenon.

## Possible Approaches

- Make precise whether any tensor-factorized model could reproduce the vacuum wedge-algebra CHSH value, or whether the commuting-operator value is strictly a III₁ artifact.
- Test whether the commuting-vs-tensor distinction changes the *scaling* of the maximal violation with spacelike separation $L$ — feeding [[long-range-bell-decay]].
- Connect to the split property / nuclearity (which *does* supply an intermediate type-I factor and a tensor structure) as the bridge between the two sides.
