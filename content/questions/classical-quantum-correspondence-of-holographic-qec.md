## Statement

Problem 4.6 of [[2026-goto-rethinking-qi-gravity-fields|Goto et al.]]: if QEC is a structural feature of holography, *how quantum* is the protected information? What is the holographic counterpart of the classical-vs-quantum distinction in error-correcting codes (which information is classically protected vs. genuinely quantum-protected)?

## Why It Matters

Peripheral to the group, but the operator-algebra framing (which subalgebra is protected) touches the [[type-iii-von-neumann-algebras|von Neumann-algebra]] language the group uses, linking loosely to [[quantum-information-in-infinite-dimensions]].

## What We Know

- Classical codes protect classical bits; quantum codes protect against a full (Pauli) error set.
- In holography the "code subspace" is the reconstructible bulk EFT sector; its classical vs. quantum content is not sharply characterized.

## Possible Approaches

- Decompose the protected algebra into classical (center) and quantum parts.
- Relate to OAQECC and to the type of the reconstructed algebra.
