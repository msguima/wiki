## Collaboration

Marcela Peláez is based at the Universidad de la República, Montevideo, Uruguay. The collaboration began in 2022. One joint paper has been published so far: Phys. Rev. D 109 (2024), focused on the ghost-gluon vertex at general kinematics in the refined Gribov-Zwanziger (RGZ) theory. This makes her the group's primary international collaborator in Uruguay.

## Joint Work

- **Ghost-gluon vertex at general kinematics in RGZ theory** (Phys. Rev. D 109, 2024): the single joint publication to date. The paper studies the ghost-gluon vertex beyond the Taylor kinematics limit within the RGZ framework, connecting the Gribov-horizon-modified propagators to the vertex structure that governs the running coupling in Landau gauge. This work builds on the [[gribov-zwanziger|Gribov-Zwanziger]] and RGZ actions developed in the longer-standing joint work with [[leticia-palhares]] and [[silvio-paolo-sorella]].

## Expertise

- Ghost and gluon propagators in Landau gauge
- Ghost-gluon vertex at general kinematics
- Dyson-Schwinger equations and Schwinger-Dyson methods
- RGZ framework and non-perturbative QCD
- Running coupling constant in the infrared
