## Overview

UERJ — Universidade do Estado do Rio de Janeiro — is the primary institutional home of the research group. The relevant unit is the **Departamento de Física Teórica** (Department of Theoretical Physics), which hosts the majority of collaborators, students, and postdocs. UERJ is a state university funded by the Rio de Janeiro state government. The department has a long-standing strength in non-perturbative QCD, gauge theory, and more recently quantum information in QFT.

Funding programs based at UERJ include the Prociência productivity grant (three consecutive cycles: 2017–2020, 2020–2023, 2023–2026), the FAPERJ Emerging Groups grant, and the FAPERJ Young Scientist grant.

## Research Focus

The UERJ group's research divides into two main programs:

1. **Quantum information in QFT** (primary program, 2023–present): Bell-CHSH and Mermin inequality violations, [[araki-uhlmann-relative-entropy|Araki-Uhlmann relative entropy]], and [[entanglement-embezzlement|entanglement embezzlement]] using [[weyl-operators|Weyl operators]] and [[tomita-takesaki-modular-theory|Tomita-Takesaki modular theory]] in algebraic QFT. See [[bell-inequalities-qft]] and [[relative-entropy-qft]].

2. **[[gribov-zwanziger|Gribov-Zwanziger physics]]** (long-standing program, 2010–present): Non-perturbative structure of non-abelian gauge theories, [[gribov-copies|Gribov copies]], [[brst-symmetry|BRST symmetry]], spectral functions, ghost-gluon vertex, and confinement phases. See [[gribov-zwanziger]] and [[confinement-duality]].

The department collaborates closely with [[cbpf]] (also in Rio de Janeiro) and with [[ghent-university]] through the CAPES-PrInt program.
