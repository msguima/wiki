## Overview

Ghent University is a major Belgian research university. The collaboration involves the **KU Leuven campus** specifically, where [[david-dudal]] is based. It is the group's primary European institutional partner. The collaboration is supported by the CAPES-PrInt program "Strong Interactions: Theoretical Foundations and Applications," a Brazilian federal program for international scientific partnerships.

Ghent University also participated in a **cotutelle PhD arrangement**: Duifje van Egmond completed a jointly supervised PhD (2020) under the co-supervision of [[david-dudal]], representing the deepest form of the institutional collaboration.

## Researchers

- [[david-dudal]] — primary collaborator at Ghent; 20+ joint papers since 2010; co-supervisor of cotutelle PhD (2020).

## Research Focus

The Ghent collaboration spans two distinct but related research programs:

1. **[[gribov-zwanziger|Gribov physics and spectral functions]]**: the core of the long-standing collaboration since 2010. Topics include the Gribov-Zwanziger and refined Gribov-Zwanziger (RGZ) frameworks, [[gribov-copies|Gribov copies]] and the [[gribov-horizon|Gribov horizon]] in Landau gauge, gluon and ghost propagators, spectral functions, glueball masses, and Higgs-confinement phases. This work falls within the [[gribov-zwanziger]] and [[confinement-duality]] areas.

2. **[[bell-inequalities-qft|Bell-CHSH in gauge theories]]**: more recent joint work on formulating Bell-CHSH inequalities consistently with the [[brst-symmetry|BRST symmetry]] structure of gauge-fixed non-abelian gauge theories. This represents the intersection of the quantum information program with the Gribov-BRST physics line.
