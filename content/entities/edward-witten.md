## Collaboration

Edward Witten (IAS) is not a collaborator of the group; there are no joint papers. As with [[hong-liu]], the `collaborator` role here marks a reference author. Witten is nonetheless the most influential outside author in this wiki and, by the researcher's own account, a major influence on the researcher's career. Eight of the ★ papers in the researcher's Zotero are Witten's, more than any other author (see `sources/manifest.yaml`, `influential_papers`), and the [[immersion-plan]] treats Witten as the hub of the entanglement–symmetries–holography school and the model for its writing apprenticeship.

Witten's work touches every line of the wiki:

- **Topological phases and anomalies.** *Three Lectures on Topological Phases of Matter* and *Fermion Path Integrals and Topological Phases* (both 2016, ★). *Anomaly Inflow and the η-Invariant* (with Yonekura, 2019) is a Track A deep read. The Witten effect ($q_e=n_e+\frac{\theta}{2\pi}n_m$) organizes Week 12 of the generalized-symmetries course ([[week-12-theta-terms-witten-effect]]) and underlies [[axionic-electrodynamics]].
- **Chern–Simons theory and the quantum Hall effect.** *Quantum Field Theory and the Jones Polynomial* (1989), and the expository [[2026-witten-chern-simons-quantum-hall]] (2026), ingested here: [[chern-simons-theory]], [[quantum-hall-effect]].
- **Entanglement and algebras in QFT.** The APS Medal article on entanglement properties of QFT (RMP 90, 2018, ★) is the main reference of the group's notes on Summers' Bell-CHSH work. *A Mini-Introduction to Information Theory* (2020, ★).
- **Crossed products and gravity.** *Gravity and the Crossed Product* and *Why Does QFT in Curved Spacetime Make Sense?* (2022, both ★), together with the Chandrasekaran–Longo–Penington–Witten and Chandrasekaran–Penington–Witten papers. These are the spine of Semester II of the algebraic-QFT course and the frontier the embezzlement program borders ([[crossed-product-construction]], [[crossed-product-and-island-formula]]).
- **Holography.** *Anti de Sitter Space and Holography* (1998), the [[gkp-witten-formula|GKP–Witten formula]].
- **Other ★ papers.** *Light Rays, Singularities, and All That* (RMP 92, 2020) and *Introduction to Black Hole Thermodynamics* (2025).

## Joint Work

No joint publications. Witten's papers enter the wiki as sources: [[2026-witten-chern-simons-quantum-hall]] so far, with the ★ papers listed above as candidates for ingestion.

## Expertise

- Topological quantum field theory: Chern–Simons theory, knot invariants, Donaldson/Seiberg–Witten theory
- Anomalies, anomaly inflow, η-invariants, and topological phases of matter (fermionic path integrals, time reversal, θ = π)
- The θ-angle and the Witten effect; electric–magnetic duality and S-duality
- Entanglement and von Neumann algebras in QFT; crossed products, observers, and generalized entropy
- String theory, M-theory, and gauge/gravity duality
- Expository writing for mathematicians: two papers in the *Bulletin of the AMS* (2007; 2026)
