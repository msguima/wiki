## Collaboration

Letícia F. Palhares is based at [[uerj]] (Departamento de Física Teórica). The collaboration began in 2013 and is ongoing, with 15+ joint papers. She holds the role of co-PI on the FAPERJ Emerging Groups grant and is a participant in the CAPES-PrInt "Strong Interactions" program, making her one of the key institutional figures in the research group.

## Joint Work

Palhares' contributions focus primarily on the non-perturbative structure of QCD and non-abelian gauge theories:

- **[[gribov-zwanziger|Gribov-Zwanziger framework]]**: joint work on the effects of [[gribov-copies|Gribov copies]] on propagators, confinement criteria, and the phase structure of gauge theories.
- **Spectral functions**: analysis of Källén-Lehmann spectral representations in the context of confinement models and the Gribov-Zwanziger action.
- **Ghost-gluon vertex**: joint work on the ghost-gluon vertex at general kinematics within the RGZ framework; this work connects to the collaboration with [[marcela-pelaez]].
- **[[confinement-duality|Phases of gauge theories]]**: collaborative investigation of Higgs-confinement phases and the order/disorder of the QCD phase diagram.
