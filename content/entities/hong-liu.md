## Collaboration

Hong Liu is a professor of physics at MIT, specializing in theoretical physics at the intersection of quantum field theory, quantum gravity, and quantum information. He is not a direct collaborator of Marcelo's group, but his work is a key reference for the quantum information program. His major review [[2025-liu-lectures-entanglement-vna|"Lectures on entanglement, von Neumann algebras, and emergence of spacetime"]] (arXiv:2510.07017, 2025) provides foundational context for [[type-iii-von-neumann-algebras|type III$_1$ von Neumann algebras]], [[tomita-takesaki-modular-theory|Tomita-Takesaki modular theory]], [[araki-uhlmann-relative-entropy|Araki-Uhlmann relative entropy]], and the [[crossed-product-construction|crossed product construction]] --- all central to Marcelo's [[bell-inequalities-qft|Bell inequality]] and [[relative-entropy-qft|relative entropy]] programs.

## Joint Work

No joint publications. The relationship is that of a key reference author whose review work provides the algebraic and gravitational context for the quantum information tools Marcelo's group applies in flat-space QFT.

## Expertise

- Von Neumann algebra classification and entanglement types
- Tomita-Takesaki modular theory and modular flows in quantum gravity
- Crossed product construction and gravitational entropy
- Algebraic formulation of AdS/CFT
- Subregion-subalgebra duality and emergent spacetime
- Non-equilibrium quantum field theory and quantum information in many-body systems
