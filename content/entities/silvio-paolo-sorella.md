## Collaboration

Silvio Paolo Sorella is based at [[uerj]] (Departamento de Física Teórica) and is the most central collaborator in the research group. The collaboration began in 2010 and has continued without interruption to the present. He functions as a senior collaborator and co-PI on multiple funded projects, and is the co-author of virtually all papers produced since 2010 — more than 50 joint publications in total. He is a co-author of every paper in the quantum information program (2023–2026).

## Joint Work

Sorella's contributions span essentially the entire research output of the group. Key areas of joint work include:

- **[[gribov-zwanziger|Gribov-Zwanziger framework]]**: long-standing collaboration on the treatment of [[gribov-copies|Gribov copies]], the [[gribov-horizon|Gribov horizon]], and the refined Gribov-Zwanziger action in non-abelian gauge theories.
- **[[brst-symmetry|BRST symmetry]]**: algebraic treatment of gauge symmetry, Faddeev-Popov zero modes, and BRST-invariant formulations of physical observables.
- **[[bell-inequalities-qft|Bell inequalities in QFT]]**: co-author of all 21+ papers in this line, including the BRST-invariant Bell-CHSH formulation and the [[weyl-operators|Weyl operator]] / [[tomita-takesaki-modular-theory|Tomita-Takesaki]] framework.
- **[[relative-entropy-qft|Relative entropy program]]**: all papers computing [[araki-uhlmann-relative-entropy|Araki-Uhlmann relative entropy]] between coherent, squeezed, and cat states.

Joint funding includes multiple cycles of the UERJ Prociência program, the FAPERJ Emerging Groups grant, and the CAPES-PrInt "Strong Interactions" program.
