## Definition

The gravitational replica trick computes Rényi entropies $S_n = (1-n)^{-1}\log(Z_n/Z_1^n)$ as ratios of bulk partition functions on $n$-fold replicated geometries with conical singularities along the entangling surface; the analytic continuation $n\to 1$ recovers the von Neumann entropy and (Lewkowycz–Maldacena) gives the RT/HRT formula. *Replica wormholes* — connected bulk saddles dominating for evaporating black holes — are responsible for the Page curve.

## Role in Research

The gravitational replica trick appears at Sem II Wks 4 and 10. It bridges the clean RT formula (static, geometric) to the black-hole information program (where replica wormholes are essential for restoring unitarity and producing the descending branch of the [[page-curve]]).
