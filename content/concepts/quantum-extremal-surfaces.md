## Definition

The quantum extremal surface (QES) prescription generalises the Ryu-Takayanagi / HRT formula by replacing the area functional with the generalised entropy: $S(A) = \min\text{ext}_\gamma\,[\text{Area}(\gamma)/(4G_N) + S_{\text{bulk}}(\Sigma_\gamma)]$, where $\Sigma_\gamma$ is the bulk region between $A$ and $\gamma$. Bulk-matter entanglement entropy enters on equal footing with area. The QES gives the island formula in the presence of a bath.

## Role in Research

The QES prescription enters the [[courses/ads-cft-course/syllabus|holography course]] with the entanglement wedges of [[lecture-19-entanglement-wedges|Lecture 19]] and is used for the island of [[lecture-26-island-calculation|Lecture 26]]. It provides the mechanism by which the [[page-curve]] is reproduced from semiclassical gravity, via islands that appear at late times and cause the entropy to decrease.
