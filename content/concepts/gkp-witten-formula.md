## Definition

The Gubser-Klebanov-Polyakov-Witten (GKP-W) formula is the operational statement of the holographic dictionary: $Z_{\text{CFT}}[J] = Z_{\text{gravity}}[\phi_\partial = J]$, equating the CFT generating functional with sources $J$ to the on-shell gravity partition function with boundary conditions $\phi_\partial = J$. The bulk field $\phi$ near the boundary has the leading expansion $\phi(z,x) \sim z^{d-\Delta}J(x) + z^\Delta\langle\mathcal{O}(x)\rangle + \cdots$ for an operator $\mathcal{O}$ of dimension $\Delta$.

## Role in Research

The GKP-W formula is the foundational machinery of Block B Wk 8 of the AdS/CFT course, providing the basis for all subsequent boundary-correlator computations in holography. It operationalizes the [[holographic-dictionary]] by giving a precise prescription for extracting CFT data from bulk solutions.
