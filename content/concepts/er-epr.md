## Definition

**ER=EPR** (Maldacena–Susskind 2013) is the conjecture that an **Einstein–Rosen bridge** (a wormhole, "ER") between two regions is the geometric manifestation of **quantum entanglement** ("EPR", after Einstein–Podolsky–Rosen) between the degrees of freedom in those regions. In its sharpest form: two entangled black holes are connected by a wormhole; more generally, spacetime connectivity is *built from* entanglement, and disentangling the two sides pinches the bridge off.

The cleanest instance is the [[thermofield-double-state|thermofield double]]: maximal $L$–$R$ entanglement of two CFTs ↔ the smooth two-sided eternal black hole. Ryu–Takayanagi ([[ryu-takayanagi-formula]]) and its quantum-extremal refinement ([[quantum-extremal-surfaces]]) make "area ↔ entanglement" quantitative; ER=EPR is the slogan-level statement of the same idea applied to connectivity rather than just entropy.

There is an **algebraic version** (developed in [[2025-liu-lectures-entanglement-vna|Liu's lectures]]): bulk connectivity is encoded in the commutant relations of the boundary subalgebras. Two regions are "connected" when their algebras fail to commute in the appropriate sense; modular flow of one boundary algebra generates geometric flow across the bridge.

## Role in Research

ER=EPR is the conceptual hinge between the group's quantum-information tools and holographic geometry:

- It says the *entanglement* the group already quantifies (via [[bell-chsh-inequality|Bell-CHSH]] violation and [[araki-uhlmann-relative-entropy|relative entropy]]) is the very thing that "is" the wormhole. So a Bell-CHSH measurement between the two boundary algebras is, under ER=EPR, a probe of **spacetime connectivity** — this is the motivation behind [[bell-chsh-in-holographic-setting]] and the [[holographic-bell-program]] connection.
- The **algebraic** form (commutant structure ↔ connectivity) is stated in exactly the [[type-iii-von-neumann-algebras|type III₁]] / [[tomita-takesaki-modular-theory|modular]] language the group is fluent in, making it a natural target rather than a foreign framework.
- It frames the [[traversable-wormholes|traversable wormhole]] puzzle: if entanglement *is* the bridge, what does it mean to *send a signal across* it (GJW), and how does that coexist with the no-signaling that Bell-CHSH presumes? See [[bell-chsh-across-traversable-wormhole]].

## Notes

ER=EPR is a *conjecture / organizing principle* (epistemic status: EXPECTED, not PROVED). Its value for this wiki is heuristic: it licenses the question "what does a flat-space entanglement measure say about bulk geometry?" without committing to a derivation. The algebraic reformulation is the part most likely to become rigorous, and is where the group's tools have traction.
