## Definition

The **Haag–Kastler axioms** (algebraic quantum field theory, AQFT) formalize a QFT not through fields acting on a fixed Hilbert space but through the assignment of a **von Neumann algebra $\mathcal{A}(\mathcal{O})$ of observables to each bounded spacetime region $\mathcal{O}$** — a *net of local algebras* $\mathcal{O}\mapsto\mathcal{A}(\mathcal{O})$. The physical content is encoded in structural properties the net must satisfy:

1. **Isotony.** $\mathcal{O}_1\subseteq\mathcal{O}_2 \Rightarrow \mathcal{A}(\mathcal{O}_1)\subseteq\mathcal{A}(\mathcal{O}_2)$ — larger regions carry more observables.
2. **Microcausality (locality).** If $\mathcal{O}_1$ and $\mathcal{O}_2$ are spacelike separated, then $[\mathcal{A}(\mathcal{O}_1),\mathcal{A}(\mathcal{O}_2)]=0$ — spacelike-separated observables commute.
3. **Poincaré covariance.** A unitary representation $U(g)$ of the Poincaré group acts geometrically, $U(g)\mathcal{A}(\mathcal{O})U(g)^{-1}=\mathcal{A}(g\mathcal{O})$.
4. **Spectrum condition.** The joint spectrum of the energy–momentum generators lies in the closed forward light cone (positivity of energy), and there is a unique Poincaré-invariant **vacuum** $|\Omega\rangle$.
5. **Additivity / completeness** (and often **weak additivity**): the global algebra is generated by the local ones.

From these, deeper structural theorems follow — **Haag duality** $\mathcal{A}(\mathcal{O})' = \mathcal{A}(\mathcal{O}')$ (the commutant of a region's algebra is the algebra of its causal complement), the **[[reeh-schlieder-theorem|Reeh–Schlieder]]** cyclicity/separability of the vacuum, the **[[bisognano-wichmann-theorem|Bisognano–Wichmann]]** identification of wedge modular flow with a boost, and the generic **[[type-iii-von-neumann-algebras|type III₁]]** classification of the local algebras. In this framework a theory is genuinely *local* only if it can be presented as a net of type III₁ factors — a criterion that, e.g., excludes topological QFT (no non-trivial local observables).

## Role in Research

The Haag–Kastler framework is the axiomatic bedrock on which the group's whole quantum-information-in-QFT program is built. It is the setting in which the recurring objects are *defined*, not merely assumed:

- **It is where "local algebra" means something.** Every statement about [[rindler-wedges|wedge]] and [[causal-diamonds|causal-diamond]] algebras, their [[type-iii-von-neumann-algebras|type III₁]] classification, and [[tomita-takesaki-modular-theory|modular theory]] presupposes the Haag–Kastler net structure.
- **It supplies the hypotheses of the key theorems.** [[reeh-schlieder-theorem|Reeh–Schlieder]] and [[bisognano-wichmann-theorem|Bisognano–Wichmann]] — the two pillars behind the [[bell-inequalities-qft|Bell-CHSH]], [[relative-entropy-qft|relative-entropy]], and [[magic-nonstabilizerness|magic]] results — are theorems *within* the Haag–Kastler axioms.
- **It defines the "local QFT vs. TQFT" boundary** invoked by [[2026-benedetti-magic-in-qft|Benedetti–Dabholkar–Dalmonte]]: locality = a net of type III₁ factors, which is exactly the property a stabilizer/TQFT vacuum lacks.
- **It is where the Gribov frontier bites.** Whether the [[refined-gribov-zwanziger|RGZ]] theory, with its non-local horizon restriction, even satisfies the Haag–Kastler axioms is an open and load-bearing question ([[entanglement-as-confinement-probe]], [[relative-entropy-interacting-theories]], [[magic-and-the-gribov-horizon]]).
- **Course backbone.** The axioms are the organizing principle of the group's AQFT course (`wiki/courses/2026-algebraic-qft-course/`).

## Notes

- "Local" in the Haag–Kastler sense is stronger than "has a local Lagrangian": it demands a non-trivial net of local observable algebras. Chern–Simons / topological theories have local Lagrangians but are **not** local nets — the distinction [[2026-benedetti-magic-in-qft|the magic paper]] uses to separate local QFT from TQFT.
- Haag duality can fail in the presence of superselection sectors or spontaneously broken symmetry (it becomes the Doplicher–Haag–Roberts / split-property analysis); for the vacuum sector of standard theories it holds and underwrites the modular-conjugation map used in the Bell-CHSH construction.
- The **split property** and **nuclearity** are refinements (Buchholz–Wichmann) controlling the local degrees of freedom; they are the technical bridge between the abstract net and lattice/continuum-limit statements relevant to the [[condensed-matter-qft-bridge|condensed-matter bridge]].
