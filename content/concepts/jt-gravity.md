## Definition

Jackiw-Teitelboim (JT) gravity is a 2-dimensional dilaton-gravity theory with bulk action $I = -\frac{1}{16\pi G_N}\int d^2x\sqrt{-g}\,\phi(R + 2/L^2)$ plus a Gibbons-Hawking boundary term, in nearly-AdS₂. On-shell dynamics reduces to a Schwarzian theory of the boundary reparametrisation mode. JT is the soluble laboratory for nearly-AdS₂ holography and the cleanest setting for Page-curve / island calculations.

## Role in Research

JT gravity is introduced in Sem I Wk 15 and returned to as the workhorse of Sem II Wk 6 (Page curve in JT + bath). Its exact solvability makes it ideal for explicit calculations of the [[page-curve]] via the [[quantum-extremal-surfaces|island prescription]], complementing the general arguments made with the full QES formula.
