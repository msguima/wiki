## Definition

The holographic dictionary is the operational map between a $d$-dimensional CFT (the "boundary") and a $(d+1)$-dimensional gravity theory on asymptotically AdS spacetime (the "bulk") asserted by [[gauge-gravity-duality|gauge/gravity duality]]. It pairs every CFT operator $\mathcal{O}$ of dimension $\Delta$ with a bulk field $\phi$ of mass $m^2 L^2 = \Delta(\Delta-d)$, identifies boundary sources with bulk boundary conditions via the [[gkp-witten-formula]], and maps CFT correlators to bulk on-shell actions.

## Role in Research

The dictionary is the central organizing apparatus of Block B of the AdS/CFT course. In the wiki, it is the bridge from the algebraic-QFT-side type III₁ structure (active research) to the bulk geometric picture of black-hole entropy.

## Relations

- [[gkp-witten-formula]] — the formal statement equating CFT generating functional with bulk on-shell action.
- [[large-n-factorization]] — the boundary-side fact that makes the bulk-side classical-gravity limit possible.
- [[ryu-takayanagi-formula]] — the entropy entry in the dictionary.
- [[subregion-subalgebra-duality]] — the algebraic refinement of bulk reconstruction.
