## Definition

In a $\mathrm{U}(N)$ or $\mathrm{SU}(N)$ gauge theory at large $N$, single-trace operators behave as generalized free fields: higher correlators factorize as if the operators were Gaussian random variables at leading order, with $1/N^2$ corrections. The algebra of single-trace operators on a boundary subregion of a holographic CFT becomes type III₁ in the $N \to \infty$ limit — the algebraic signature of emergent semiclassical bulk geometry.
