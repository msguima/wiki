## Definition

The Page curve is the time-dependence of the fine-grained entanglement entropy of Hawking radiation predicted by unitary evaporation of a black hole: rising from zero, peaking at the Page time (when radiation entropy equals remaining black-hole entropy), then falling back to zero as the black hole fully evaporates. Reproducing it from a semiclassical gravity calculation requires the quantum extremal surface / island prescription, with replica wormholes responsible for the descending branch.

## Role in Research

The Page curve is the central physical object that the second half of the AdS/CFT course is organised around, covered in Sem II Block 2 Wk 9. Its derivation from semiclassical gravity — via [[quantum-extremal-surfaces]] and [[replica-trick-gravity|replica wormholes]] — is one of the main results of the modern black-hole information program.

## Relations

- [[quantum-extremal-surfaces]] — the QES / island prescription reproduces the Page curve from semiclassical gravity.
- [[replica-trick-gravity]] — replica wormholes are responsible for the descending branch of the curve.
- [[jt-gravity]] — JT gravity + bath is the simplest system in which the Page curve can be derived explicitly.
