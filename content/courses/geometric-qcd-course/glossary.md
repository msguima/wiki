# Glossary

**Area derivative.** An oriented infinitesimal contour-area insertion. In the ordered holonomy model it inserts curvature with its surrounding transport. Its singular continuum domain must be specified.

**Loop operator.** The composition $\mathcal L_\nu=\Delta_\mu A_{\mu\nu}$. Its product rule is conditional on the regulated continuity assumptions explained in I.4.

**Planar factorization.** Leading factorization of expectation values of products of normalized single traces in the usual large-$N_c$ expansion. It is not multiplicativity of one trace.

**Ordered integral.** An integral over ordered contour parameters. Products satisfy shuffle relations. Closedness kills its first level but generally leaves a nonzero antisymmetric second level.

**Logarithmic Magnus series.** A Lie series for the logarithm of an ordered exponential. It differs from the tensor expansion of the exponential itself.

**Pairing basis.** Candidate invariant tensors built from Kronecker-delta pairings. The count $(n-1)!!$ precedes cyclic and dimension-dependent relations and is not an equation rank.

**Inconsistency certificate.** An exact witness such as $y^TA=0$, $y^Tb\ne0$. It certifies failure of the stated system, not every possible ansatz.

**Free-word representation.** Operators creating and removing letters in a full Fock basis. Creation operators do not commute, and the vacuum state is not a trace.

**Self-dual two-form.** A Euclidean four-dimensional two-form satisfying $*B=B$. A nonzero real simple tangent bivector cannot have this property.

**Hodge-dual surface proposal.** The source's extended area construction. Its literal tensor formula fails the circle test in III.5, so dependent QCD conclusions remain unresolved.

**Harmonic versus conformal.** Harmonicity solves a Laplace equation; conformality constrains the induced metric. A Hilbert-transform extension of a fixed ellipse parametrization illustrates the difference.

**Goldschmidt branch.** A disconnected or pinched spanning-surface candidate whose area is additive in the prescribed pieces. Its selection in a QCD ansatz is additional input.

**Majorana Pfaffian.** The Gaussian integral of a real antisymmetric fermion quadratic form. Its square is a determinant; its sign requires a convention.

**Connected loop sum.** A trace logarithm. Exponentiation produces disconnected collections in the partition function.

**Approximate identity.** A normalized family of kernels converging against test functions to a delta distribution. Localization alone does not fix normalization.

**Exact WKB.** A claim that a semiclassical quantization procedure receives no spectral corrections in the stated setting. It is conjectured for the source construction, not established by a steep Hessian alone.

**In-sample fit.** Comparison with data used to tune the parameters. A held-out prediction uses data reserved before fitting, without subsequent retuning.
