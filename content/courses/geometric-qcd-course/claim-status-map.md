## Dependency of the open claims

The geometric problem in III.5 affects the proposed zero-mode realization and subsequent surface-fermion QCD identification. It does not invalidate the independent finite calculations in the course. The eighth-order algebra and the meson fit require their own reproducibility work; neither can establish the missing geometric identity.

The source PDF and published/preprint records are preserved as evidence. A future repair must state which definition or assumption changes and rerun the affected arguments; it must not erase the counterexample by silently changing conventions.
