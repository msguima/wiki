## Earlier bibliography and audit history

> **Status note (2026-09-02).** Rebuilt after an audit found the appendix carried 12 entries while the thirty notes cited 43 papers between them — the twenty-one missing ones included the anchors of entire weeks (CHM for Sem II Wk 3, Engelhardt–Wall for Wk 5, AMMZ for Wk 11, BFLW for Wk 13). All are now listed. A second pass on 2026-09-02 caught ten more — every one an old-style `hep-th/NNNNNNN` identifier, which the first automated check had silently skipped over; those included the anchor papers for Week 9 (Skenderis) and Sem II Wk 12 (Maldacena's eternal black holes). This matters more than it did in May: with the course moving to a **self-contained** footing rather than a scaffold over adscft.org, this appendix, and no longer the site's per-chapter reading lists, is the course's actual reading spine.

**Citation policy.** Entries give the arXiv identifier, which is the stable primary reference and the one to verify against. A journal reference is included where one has been checked; its absence means it has not been checked, and never that the paper is unpublished. Cite by arXiv ID in the notes.

## A. Foundational AdS/CFT (Sem I Block B background)

- **Maldacena, *The large-N limit of superconformal field theories and supergravity*** — Adv. Theor. Math. Phys. 2 (1998) 231, [arXiv:hep-th/9711200](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9711200). The original conjecture.
- **Gubser–Klebanov–Polyakov, *Gauge theory correlators from non-critical string theory*** — Phys. Lett. B 428 (1998) 105, [arXiv:hep-th/9802109](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9802109). One half of the dictionary.
- **Witten, *Anti-de Sitter space and holography*** — Adv. Theor. Math. Phys. 2 (1998) 253, [arXiv:hep-th/9802150](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9802150). The other half; the generating-functional formulation.

## B. Reviews and lecture notes

- **Aharony–Gubser–Maldacena–Ooguri–Oz, *Large $N$ field theories, string theory and gravity*** — Phys. Rep. 323 (2000) 183, [arXiv:hep-th/9905111](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9905111). The canonical review of the foundations.
- **McGreevy, *Holographic duality with a view toward many-body physics*** — Adv. High Energy Phys. 2010, 723105, [arXiv:0909.0518](https://arxiv.org/abs/0909.0518). The "view from physics" entry point.
- **Harlow, *TASI Lectures on the emergence of bulk physics in AdS/CFT*** — PoS TASI2017 (2018) 002, [arXiv:1802.01040](https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.01040). Modern, algebraic-flavoured.
- **Penedones, *TASI lectures on AdS/CFT*** — [arXiv:1608.04948](https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.04948). Witten diagrams and Mellin space. *Cited in Sem I Wk 10, Sem II Wk 2.*
- **Liu, *Lectures on entanglement, von Neumann algebras, and emergence of spacetime*** — [arXiv:2510.07017](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.07017), 2025. The bridge to the algebraic-QFT machinery. *Already ingested:* [[2025-liu-lectures-entanglement-vna]].

## C. CFT background (Sem I Block A)

- **Rychkov, *EPFL Lectures on Conformal Field Theory in $D\ge3$ Dimensions*** — [arXiv:1601.05000](https://arxiv.org/abs/1601.05000). The conformal algebra, radial quantisation, the full unitarity-bound proof. *Cited in Wks 1, 2.*
- **Simmons-Duffin, *TASI Lectures on the Conformal Bootstrap*** — [arXiv:1602.07982](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.07982). Radial quantisation in explicit formulas (§2); blocks and crossing (§3). *Cited in Wks 2, 3.*
- **Pappadopulo–Rychkov–Espin–Rattazzi, *OPE convergence in conformal field theory*** — [arXiv:1202.2064](https://arxiv.org/abs/1202.2064). The quantitative convergence statement Week 3 turns on. *Cited in Wk 3.*
- **Belavin–Polyakov–Zamolodchikov, *Infinite conformal symmetry in two-dimensional quantum field theory*** — Nucl. Phys. B241 (1984) 333. The founding 2d paper; no arXiv entry. *Cited in Wk 5.*
- **Strominger, *Black hole entropy from near-horizon microstates*** — [arXiv:hep-th/9712251](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9712251). The Cardy count of BTZ entropy that Week 5 §4 reproduces. *Cited in Wk 5.*

Books used without arXiv entries: **di Francesco–Mathieu–Sénéchal**, *Conformal Field Theory* (Wk 5); **Polchinski**, *String Theory* Vol. 1 (Wk 1).

## D. Correlators, Witten diagrams, Mellin space (Sem I Wk 10, Sem II Wk 2)

- **Freedman–Mathur–Matusis–Rastelli (FMMR), *Correlation functions in the CFT(d)/AdS(d+1) correspondence*** — [arXiv:hep-th/9804058](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9804058). The original two- and three-point Witten-diagram computations. *Cited in Sem I Wk 10.*
- **D'Hoker–Freedman, *Supersymmetric gauge theories and the AdS/CFT correspondence*** — [arXiv:hep-th/0201253](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0201253). $D$-functions and exchange diagrams. *Cited in Sem I Wk 10, Sem II Wk 2.*
- **Klebanov–Witten, *AdS/CFT correspondence and symmetry breaking*** — [arXiv:hep-th/9905104](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9905104). Alternative quantisation and the $\Delta_-$ branch. *Cited in Sem I Wk 8.*
- **Penedones, *Writing CFT correlation functions as AdS scattering amplitudes*** — [arXiv:1011.1485](https://arxiv.org/abs/1011.1485). Mellin amplitudes and the flat-space limit. *Cited in Sem II Wk 2.*

## E. Holographic renormalisation (Sem I Wk 9, Sem II Wk 1)

- **de Haro–Skenderis–Solodukhin, *Holographic reconstruction of spacetime and renormalization in the AdS/CFT correspondence*** — [arXiv:hep-th/0002230](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0002230). The systematic Fefferman–Graham and holographic-stress-tensor procedure. *Cited in Sem I Wk 9, Sem II Wk 1.*
- **Skenderis, *Lecture notes on holographic renormalization*** — [arXiv:hep-th/0209067](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0209067). The canonical review; scheme equivalence. *Cited in Sem I Wk 9, Sem II Wk 1.*

## F. Holographic entanglement entropy (Sem I Block C, Sem II Block 1)

- **Ryu–Takayanagi, *Holographic derivation of entanglement entropy from AdS/CFT*** — PRL 96 (2006) 181602, [arXiv:hep-th/0603001](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0603001).
- **Calabrese–Cardy, *Entanglement entropy and quantum field theory*** — [arXiv:hep-th/0405152](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0405152). The 2d CFT result Week 13 reproduces holographically. *Cited in Wk 13.*
- **Hubeny–Rangamani–Takayanagi, *A covariant holographic entanglement entropy proposal*** — JHEP 07 (2007) 062, [arXiv:0705.0016](https://arxiv.org/abs/0705.0016). Covariant (HRT) version.
- **Lewkowycz–Maldacena, *Generalized gravitational entropy*** — JHEP 08 (2013) 090, [arXiv:1304.4926](https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.4926). Derivation via the replica trick.
- **Dong, *Holographic entanglement entropy for general higher derivative gravity*** — [arXiv:1310.5713](https://arxiv.org/abs/1310.5713). *Cited in Sem II Wk 4.*
- **Jafferis–Lewkowycz–Maldacena–Suh (JLMS), *Relative entropy equals bulk relative entropy*** — JHEP 06 (2016) 004, [arXiv:1512.06431](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.06431). Subregion–subalgebra duality at the algebraic level.
- **Faulkner–Lewkowycz–Maldacena (FLM), *Quantum corrections to holographic entanglement entropy*** — [arXiv:1307.2892](https://arxiv.org/abs/1307.2892). The $1/N$ correction. *Cited in Sem II Wk 5.*
- **Engelhardt–Wall (EW), *Quantum extremal surfaces: holographic entanglement entropy beyond the classical regime*** — [arXiv:1408.3203](https://arxiv.org/abs/1408.3203). The QES prescription. *Cited in Sem II Wk 5.*

## G. Modular flow and the entanglement first law (Sem II Wk 3)

- **Casini–Huerta–Myers (CHM), *Towards a derivation of holographic entanglement entropy*** — JHEP 05 (2011) 036, [arXiv:1102.0440](https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.0440). The ball modular Hamiltonian and the conformal map to the hyperbolic cylinder. **The anchor for Sem II Wk 3 §3.**
- **Faulkner–Guica–Hartman–Myers–Van Raamsdonk (FGHMV), *Gravitation from entanglement in holographic CFTs*** — [arXiv:1312.7856](https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.7856). Bulk Killing flow as the dual of boundary modular flow; the first law giving linearised Einstein equations. Distinct from FLM (§F) despite the overlapping authors.
- **Faulkner–Leigh–Parrikar–Wang (FLPW), *Modular Hamiltonians for deformed half-spaces and the averaged null energy condition*** — [arXiv:1605.08072](https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.08072). *Cited in Sem II Wk 13.*

## H. Energy conditions and focusing (Sem II Wk 13)

- **Bousso–Fisher–Leichenauer–Wall (BFLW), *A quantum focusing conjecture*** — [arXiv:1506.02669](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.02669). The QFC and the QNEC. **The primary source for Sem II Wk 13, which has no adscft.org counterpart.**
- **Harlow–Ooguri, *Symmetries in quantum field theory and quantum gravity*** — [arXiv:1810.05338](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.05338). No global symmetries in quantum gravity. *Cited in Sem II Wk 15.*

## I. Probes: Wilson loops and thermal holography (Sem I Wks 11–12)

- **Maldacena, *Wilson loops in large N field theories*** — [arXiv:hep-th/9803002](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9803002). The original heavy-quark potential. *Cited in Sem I Wk 11.*
- **Witten, *Anti-de Sitter space, thermal phase transition, and confinement in gauge theories*** — [arXiv:hep-th/9803131](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9803131). Hawking–Page as deconfinement. *Cited in Sem I Wk 12.*

## J. Black-hole information (Sem II Block 2)

- **Mathur, *The information paradox: a pedagogical introduction*** — [arXiv:0909.1038](https://arxiv.org/abs/0909.1038). *Cited in Sem II Wk 8.*
- **Page, *Average entropy of a subsystem*** — [arXiv:gr-qc/9305007](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9305007), and ***Information in black hole radiation*** — [arXiv:hep-th/9306083](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9306083). The two papers behind the Page curve. *Cited in Sem II Wk 9.*
- **Penington, *Entanglement wedge reconstruction and the information paradox*** — JHEP 09 (2020) 002, [arXiv:1905.08255](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.08255).
- **Almheiri–Engelhardt–Marolf–Maxfield (AEMM), *The entropy of bulk quantum fields and the entanglement wedge of an evaporating black hole*** — JHEP 12 (2019) 063, [arXiv:1905.08762](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.08762).
- **Almheiri–Mahajan–Maldacena–Zhao (AMMZ), *The Page curve of Hawking radiation from semiclassical geometry*** — JHEP 03 (2020) 149, [arXiv:1908.10996](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.10996). The island formula in its current form, with the JT toy model. **The anchor paper for Sem II Wk 11.**
- **Almheiri–Mahajan–Maldacena, *Islands outside the horizon*** — [arXiv:1910.11077](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.11077). Locates the island boundary just outside the horizon. *Cited in Sem II Wk 11.*
- **Almheiri–Hartman–Maldacena–Shaghoulian–Tajdini (AHMST), *Replica wormholes and the entropy of Hawking radiation*** — JHEP 05 (2020) 013, [arXiv:1911.12333](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.12333).
- **Almheiri–Hartman–Maldacena–Shaghoulian–Tajdini, *The entropy of Hawking radiation*** — Rev. Mod. Phys. 93 (2021) 035002, [arXiv:2006.06872](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.06872). The review; §4 is the canonical pedagogical version of the island calculation.
- **Penington–Shenker–Stanford–Yang (PSSY), *Replica wormholes and the black hole interior*** — JHEP 03 (2022) 205, [arXiv:1911.11977](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.11977).

## K. ER=EPR and traversable wormholes (Sem II Wks 12, 14)
