# Bibliography and Paper Map

The canonical bibliography for the course. Papers listed below are **candidates for paper-page ingestion** (via the playbook's Ingest workflow) as the course progresses; in Phase 1, only the existing [[2025-liu-lectures-entanglement-vna]] page exists.

When a paper is ingested as a full `wiki/papers/` page, the entry below is updated to use `[[wikilink]]` syntax.

---

## A. Foundational AdS/CFT (Sem I Block B background)

- **Maldacena, *The large-N limit of superconformal field theories and supergravity*** — Adv. Theor. Math. Phys. 2 (1998) 231, [arXiv:hep-th/9711200](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9711200). The original conjecture.
- **Gubser–Klebanov–Polyakov, *Gauge theory correlators from non-critical string theory*** — Phys. Lett. B 428 (1998) 105, [arXiv:hep-th/9802109](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9802109). One half of the dictionary.
- **Witten, *Anti-de Sitter space and holography*** — Adv. Theor. Math. Phys. 2 (1998) 253, [arXiv:hep-th/9802150](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9802150). The other half; the generating-functional formulation.

## B. Reviews

- **Aharony–Gubser–Maldacena–Ooguri–Oz, *Large $N$ field theories, string theory and gravity*** — Phys. Rep. 323 (2000) 183, [arXiv:hep-th/9905111](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9905111). The canonical review of the foundations.
- **McGreevy, *Holographic duality with a view toward many-body physics*** — Adv. High Energy Phys. 2010, 723105, [arXiv:0909.0518](https://arxiv.org/abs/0909.0518). The "view from physics" entry point.
- **Harlow, *TASI Lectures on the emergence of bulk physics in AdS/CFT*** — PoS TASI2017 (2018) 002, [arXiv:1802.01040](https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.01040). Modern, algebraic-flavoured.
- **Liu, *Lectures on entanglement, von Neumann algebras, and emergence of spacetime*** — [arXiv:2510.07017](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.07017), 2025. The bridge to the algebraic-QFT machinery. *Already ingested:* [[2025-liu-lectures-entanglement-vna]].

## C. Holographic entanglement entropy (Sem I Block C, Sem II Block 1)

- **Ryu–Takayanagi, *Holographic derivation of entanglement entropy from AdS/CFT*** — PRL 96 (2006) 181602, [arXiv:hep-th/0603001](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0603001).
- **Hubeny–Rangamani–Takayanagi, *A covariant holographic entanglement entropy proposal*** — JHEP 07 (2007) 062, [arXiv:0705.0016](https://arxiv.org/abs/0705.0016). Covariant (HRT) version.
- **Lewkowycz–Maldacena, *Generalized gravitational entropy*** — JHEP 08 (2013) 090, [arXiv:1304.4926](https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.4926). Derivation via the replica trick.
- **Jafferis–Lewkowycz–Maldacena–Suh, *Relative entropy equals bulk relative entropy*** — JHEP 06 (2016) 004, [arXiv:1512.06431](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.06431). Subregion-subalgebra duality at the algebraic level.

## D. Black-hole information (Sem II Block 2)

- **Penington, *Entanglement wedge reconstruction and the information paradox*** — JHEP 09 (2020) 002, [arXiv:1905.08255](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.08255).
- **Almheiri–Engelhardt–Marolf–Maxfield, *The entropy of bulk quantum fields and the entanglement wedge of an evaporating black hole*** — JHEP 12 (2019) 063, [arXiv:1905.08762](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.08762).
- **Almheiri–Hartman–Maldacena–Shaghoulian–Tajdini, *Replica wormholes and the entropy of Hawking radiation*** — JHEP 05 (2020) 013, [arXiv:1911.12333](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.12333).
- **Penington–Shenker–Stanford–Yang, *Replica wormholes and the black hole interior*** — JHEP 03 (2022) 205, [arXiv:1911.11977](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.11977).

## E. Traversable wormholes (Sem II Block 2, Wk 14)

- **Gao–Jafferis–Wall, *Traversable wormholes via a double trace deformation*** — JHEP 12 (2017) 151, [arXiv:1608.05687](https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.05687).
- **Maldacena–Qi, *Eternal traversable wormhole*** — [arXiv:1804.00491](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.00491).

## F. JT gravity and the Schwarzian (Sem I Wk 15, Sem II Wk 6)

- **Maldacena–Stanford–Yang, *Conformal symmetry and its breaking in two dimensional Nearly Anti-de-Sitter space*** — PTEP 2016, 12C104, [arXiv:1606.01857](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.01857).
- **Engelsöy–Mertens–Verlinde, *An investigation of AdS₂ backreaction and holography*** — JHEP 07 (2016) 139, [arXiv:1606.03438](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.03438).

## G. Algebraic / type-III₁ side (cross-reference to AQFT Sem II)

- **Witten, *Gravity and the crossed product*** — JHEP 10 (2022) 008, [arXiv:2112.12828](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.12828).
- **Chandrasekaran–Penington–Witten, *Large $N$ algebras and generalized entropy*** — JHEP 04 (2023) 009, [arXiv:2209.10454](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.10454).
- **Ahmad–Jefferson, *Crossed product algebras and generalized entropy for subregions*** (AAJ 2024) — [arXiv:2501.01487](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.01487).
