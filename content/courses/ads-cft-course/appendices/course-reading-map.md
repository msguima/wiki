# Reading and source map for the complete course

Edition 2.2, 1 October 2026. Read the notes' calculations first. The sources below supply theorems, broader context, or the original gravitational construction; they do not replace missing steps in a calculation the course claims to derive.

The [[opening-block-reading-map|opening map]] retains the detailed rationale for Lectures 1–4. Source records and the indicated portions of available full texts were consulted during the redesign. This is a map of the active course, not a claim to have audited every result in every cited paper.

## What students should not have to infer from a citation

A label such as “theorem” means that hypotheses and a source are given; it does not mean the course reproduces the full proof. A claimed worked calculation should be reproducible from the note itself. The free-field chain does not prove type-III classification. A graph cut does not derive a physical area. The Page formula does not specify evaporation dynamics. The affine representation does not alone construct a full local net.

For deeper research, use the [[courses/ads-cft-course/appendices/bibliography-and-paper-map|older bibliography]] as a catalogue whose historical week labels and audit status must be respected. The active route is the 32-lecture sequence, not the earlier CFT-first ordering.
